    Import overhead image

    Use a north-up Web Mercator image export that you own or may use to derive building data. Supply its outer geographic bounds. An ordinary unaligned screenshot or an image in another projection will not match footprints.

    Up to 12 MB. This imports your file locally. It does not download or analyse the satellite basemap.

    Building condition

    What state each building is in today. Evidence comes from reports, reviews, OpenStreetMap and satellite radar, each with its date and source; a building nobody has evidence for stays unknown, not intact.

    Open an export to see its condition.

    Radar estimate

    Sentinel-1 radar compares the latest weeks with the year before the invasion, for the area of the open export (any place in Ukraine, up to 250 km²). It is an estimate with low confidence: good for districts, weak for single houses. Where no published figures calibrate it, the general score lines are used.

    Reports & sources

    Headlines from GDELT about this place. Nothing is applied to a building by itself: pick a report and link it to the building it names.

    Add a report here, then use Review mode and click the building it names. Facts only: keep troop positions and operations out.
