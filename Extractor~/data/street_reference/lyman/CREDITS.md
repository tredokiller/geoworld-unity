Dates: description dates explicitly labelled as uploads remain upload dates. Other source-reported capture dates and camera EXIF readings are kept with their stated meaning, without claiming a verified camera clock or timezone. `uploadedAt` is the Commons file-version timestamp. Source-reported dates are not promoted to `capturedAt`; inspect each record's `dateMetadata` and notes.

Positions: source geotags are used only as reference locations; camera positions and directions are unverified. Photographs without returned geotags retain null positions. Building names do not establish coordinates. Observations remain unplaced until manually linked or mapped.

Observations: AI-assisted visual reviews describe visible historical appearance. Approximate colors are visual estimates, not calibrated material measurements. Hidden or complex roofs are not assigned a simple roof shape. The Peter and Paul church reference is historical; its source description reports that the building burned in 2020.
